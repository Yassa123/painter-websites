Questions

Good to know.

How much does it cost to paint a house?

Every home is different, so we give a free written estimate after a short visit. Most Huntsville homes are quoted within 48 hours, and the price on the estimate is the price you pay.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We carry general liability and workers’ compensation insurance, and we are happy to send certificates before work starts.

Do I need to move my furniture?

No. Our crew moves and covers furniture, protects floors and puts everything back when we finish.

How long does an exterior repaint take?

Most homes take three to five working days, depending on size, repairs and the weather.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes. Every job comes with a written workmanship guarantee.
